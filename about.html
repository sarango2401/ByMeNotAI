About this blog

I write here about the things I am genuinely learning — mostly science and technology, occasionally literature. The goal is to think out loud: to take something I have been studying or reading and try to explain it clearly enough that I understand it better myself.

The name ByMeNotAI is a commitment. Every word on this site was written by a human being — me — without AI assistance. In an era where generated text is everywhere, I wanted a place where the writing is slow, considered, and unmistakably mine.

What you will find here

  • Science — physics, biology, mathematics, and the ideas that connect them.
  • Technology — how computers work, why they were designed the way they were, and where they might go.
  • Literature — reflections on books, essays, and authors that shaped how I think.

Posts appear when I have something worth saying — not on a schedule. Quality over frequency.
